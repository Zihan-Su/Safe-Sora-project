The idea

Watermark protection,
built into generation.

AI-generated videos need reliable copyright protection. Embedding an invisible graphical watermark requires preserving both video quality and the recoverability of the watermark.

Safe-Sora embeds graphical watermarks directly during video generation. Adaptive patch matching aligns watermark content with video regions, while wavelet-enhanced Mamba supports embedding and recovery across frames.
